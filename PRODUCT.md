# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Students (primary):** working doctors, mainly from South Asia and the Middle East, preparing for postgraduate licensing exams (launch: MRCOG Part 1, MRCOG Part 2, AMC Part 1). They study mostly on their phones, in short bursts between shifts, and sit longer timed mocks when they can. Their job: "Show me what is likely to come up and let me practise it like the real exam", and pass.
- **Staff:** content reviewers (doctors or experts), content managers and super admins, working in Content Studio.
- Recalls Tutor is a Med Exam Expert product; the CEO of Med Exam Expert is the sponsor.

## Product Purpose
A subscription question bank built from exam recalls. Success is a student who passes, and renews or resits with us. The website must make exam preparation feel simple and manageable, and make it obvious within seconds how the platform helps a student prepare for and pass their exam.

## Positioning
"The recall QBank for postgraduate medical licensing exams, updated after every sitting." Competitors sell static banks written by authors; Recalls Tutor offers the questions students actually remembered from recent sittings, organised by sitting, with questions seen in two or more sittings marked high-yield.

## Operating Context
- **Where recalls come from (must be stated accurately):** after an exam, students discuss and share the questions they remember in study groups and online communities. Recalls Tutor collects these student-shared questions from various sources, compiles, organises and structures them, reviews them, and publishes them as practice questions. They are not official questions, do not come from exam halls and are not supplied by any exam body. Recalls Tutor is independent of every exam body.
- An AI assistant drafts questions from recalls; only a human with the right role can publish.
- Practice: Revision Mode (feedback after each answer), Exam Mode (timed, results at the end), complete papers by sitting, mistakes deck, high-yield and latest-diet packs, custom sessions by topic, sitting and difficulty; results by topic; session history.
- Plans: per exam, 3, 6 or 12 months, auto-renewing, USD; the 6-month plan is the highlighted best value. A no-card free trial per exam is the main conversion lever.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Prices are not announced yet; never show invented prices.
- Exam timings, pass marks and question counts come from the catalogue; never invent them.
- Policy pages are drafts pending legal review.
- Planned, not yet built: Pass Predictor (likelihood band), dashboard countdown and daily target, peer comparison.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Recalls Tutor. Everything else (logo, colours, type) is open for redesign as of 3 November 2026.
- The CEO's direction for the public website and student app: modern and contemporary, in line with today's educational platforms and SaaS applications; simple, approachable and easy to use; subtle gradients and other contemporary treatments are welcome. The public site and the student app share one visual identity. Content Studio is out of scope for the redesign.

## Evidence on Hand
- No testimonials, pass results, user counts or review scores exist. Do not invent any.
- Real product screens (practice session, results, session history, sample question) can be shown.
- Question and sitting counts per exam come from the live catalogue.

## Product Principles
1. Make preparation feel manageable: one clear next step, never a wall of options.
2. Be exact about where questions come from; that honesty is the brand's advantage in a category that overclaims.
3. Phone first: every core task works one-handed in a few minutes.
4. Show the product doing its job instead of claiming results.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
English UI. Readable at phone sizes in bright light; WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
