# EOD — 5 November 2026

Phase 2 is finished and most of Phase 3 is built. The app is ready to deploy as soon as the CEO's accounts and keys arrive; target ship date is 7 November.

**Backend**
- OCR: Claude reads scanned PDFs, photos and screenshots before drafting; only the cleaned text is kept.
- Partner seats: prepaid packs or monthly billing, seat balances, revoke and extend, invite links, and alerts when seats run low or a pack is about to expire.
- Partner API (`/v1`): API keys, test mode, protection against double-charging on retried requests, rate limits, and signed notifications (webhooks) with automatic retries.
- Student consent to share progress with a partner, plus a scheduled job for expiry notices.
- Coupons created in Stripe, and a revenue report by exam, sitting page, new vs renewal and coupon, including refunds and disputes, with CSV export.
- Emails: receipts, a renewal reminder, payment-failed notices, a win-back code, trial emails and a trial offer (off until a discount is set). One free trial per exam per device.
- Pass Predictor, spaced repetition (1, 3, 7 and 14 days) and a weekly study plan.
- AI helpers: papers proposed for each sitting, drafted fixes for error reports, edits proposed from new evidence, new sittings noticed in uploads, and AI cost tracking with a monthly budget alert.
- Phase 3: percentiles, a 1-month plan, adding new exam families, referrals, an AI tutor limited per hour and per day, moderated discussion, and partner white-label.
- Deployment: migrations run automatically on production deploys, scheduled jobs are configured, pooled database connections work, AI runs on the Claude API, and a readiness check lists any missing setting.

**Frontend**
- Partner Portal: overview, students and seats (including CSV upload), invite links, billing, and a developer page.
- Studio → Partners for Super Admins: approve or suspend partners, set products and seat prices, record seat packs, view partner revenue.
- Invite-link page for students, and a progress-sharing switch on the student profile.
- Studio: coupons, revenue report, offer settings, proposed edits, comment moderation, the blog, adding exams, partner branding, and AI usage on the overview.
- Students: Pass Predictor, study plan with notes download, review deck, AI tutor and discussion on each question, invite-a-friend link and offer-email switch.
- Public site: topic pages, free sample questions and the blog.

**Recommendation for hosting**
- Host on Vercel. If the current account is on the free Hobby plan, upgrade to Pro: Hobby doesn't allow commercial use, and the site takes payments.
- Use a hosted Postgres (Neon or Supabase) in the same region as the app.
