# Recalls Tutor — EOD Update, 2 November 2026

## Summary

Today we completed the remaining core content and practice features: PDF and Word uploads, topic and sitting tagging, complete exam papers, and the free trial. All automated tests (10 of 10) and the production build pass, and the main flows were checked in the browser on test data.

## Completed today

- **PDF and Word uploads:** the Recall AI Studio now accepts PDF and Word documents as well as WhatsApp/Telegram exports and text files. Unsupported files (scans, images, password-protected files) get a clear message explaining what to do.
- **Exam catalogue:** a new admin screen where the team enters each exam's real details (number of questions, duration, pass mark), its sittings and topics, approved reference sources, house style for the AI, and free-trial rules. Nothing is hard-coded.
- **Topic and sitting tagging:**
  - Every question can be tagged with a topic, a difficulty and the sittings it appeared in.
  - The AI now chooses topics from the exam's approved list, and drafts are tagged automatically with the sitting chosen at upload.
  - Retagging never removes a question from students.
- **Smarter student practice:**
  - Students can filter by topic, sitting, difficulty, high-yield and latest sitting, with one-tap "High-yield sprint" and "Latest-diet pack".
  - After answering, students see which sittings a question appeared in, with High-yield and New badges.
  - Results include a topic-by-topic breakdown, weakest topics first.
- **Complete exam papers:** content managers can build full papers from approved questions, set the order, lock them and publish them. Students see a paper library grouped by sitting and sit papers timed, like the real exam.
- **Free trial:**
  - Students can try an exam free with no card: one trial per exam, limited by days and number of questions, with one mini mock.
  - Trial questions come only from older sittings chosen by the team.
  - It is switched on per exam by a Super Admin.
- **Security improvement:** student access is now rechecked on every answer, so expired plans or trials cannot keep practising.

## Progress

The full content-to-student journey is now in place:
1. Upload recalls (chat, PDF or Word).
2. Claude drafts the questions.
3. A human reviews, tags and publishes them.
4. The team builds complete papers.
5. Students practise by topic or sitting, sit timed papers, or start a free trial.
