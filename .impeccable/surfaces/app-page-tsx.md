## Scope
Public website (home first, then exam, sitting, plans, FAQ, about, contact, policy and sign-in pages) and the student app (practice home, session, results, sessions and results, plans, account). Persuade mode on the website; Operate mode in the student app. Content Studio is out of scope.

## Audience and job
Working doctors in South Asia and the Middle East preparing for MRCOG Part 1, MRCOG Part 2 and AMC Part 1, mostly on phones between shifts. The website must show within seconds how Recalls Tutor helps them pass and make preparation feel manageable. Primary action: start the free trial for one exam. Proof is the product itself (a real sample question with its sitting tags, real counts from the catalogue). No testimonials, pass rates or prices exist.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The QBank as a pocket clinical handbook: every topic owns a coloured thumb tab cut into the page edge, so a student always knows where they are and what is left. It refuses the category default of a centred gradient hero over a row of identical feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Cool ground #f5f6fb, ink navy #14213d, white rounded pages (24-28px outer radius, 12-14px inner). Five topic tab hues as soft two-stop gradients (coral, saffron, teal, violet, sky); the brand gradient violet to sky is spent only on the single primary action and the logo. Tabs are die-cut: rounded on the outer edge only, flush against the page. Origin tags are pill tickets ("Seen in Sep 2026"), high-yield is the saffron ticket. States carry shape and label, never colour alone.
STORY: A doctor lands, reads that these are the questions students remembered from recent sittings, sees a real question with its origin tickets and tabs showing what is left, picks their exam, and starts a free trial. Further down: how recalls become questions (shared, collected, compiled, reviewed), the practice modes, the sittings, plans pending, honest independence note.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: top nav with logo and one "Start free trial" action. Left 40%: outcome headline at 64-72px, one sub-line naming the student-shared source, exam picker as tab chips, primary gradient button, a small honesty line. Right 60%: an open handbook page bleeding off the right edge, holding the sample question with origin tickets and answer options, and a vertical column of topic tabs on its outer edge with "left" counts. Phone: headline, exam chips, button, then the page with the tab column turned into a horizontal tab strip above the question.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Tapping a tab turns the handbook page to that topic (the page slides, the tab comes forward); in the student app the same tab rail is the topic navigation and coverage display. Motion is one page-turn slide; reduced motion swaps instantly.
FORM: Thumb-index handbook, my grounded candidate 4 of 7 (assigned by the roll). seed:4894e76db7f34dab. Raises: origin ticket on every question (from the night-market challenger); accent only on the one action (centre-rail); unanswered shown as visible gaps (video-store wall); topic performance as one row of identical tab meters (meter bridge); state by shape and label as well as colour (exposure record).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
